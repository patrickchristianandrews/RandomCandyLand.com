# Recorded Animal Jam audio — license and sources

Pastel v0.4 replaces all eight synthesized imitation calls with actual
recordings of animals. Source files are bundled **into the firmware during the
build** (not streamed over the internet), resampled to 16 kHz and encoded as
G.711 mu-law. No original M-VAVE factory samples are used.

The seven Freesound CC0 clips are original recorded animals taken from:
https://github.com/djfksjd/session-alarm at pinned commit
`7da7e78245025e65ae77fd9d3c87b82dad0465db`.
That project provides the exact recording title, contributor, license,
preview-file SHA256, derived WAV SHA256, original clip offset and date of
retrieval: [sources.json](https://github.com/djfksjd/session-alarm/blob/main/plugins/session-alarm/assets/sounds/sources.json)
and [SOUND_LICENSE.md](https://github.com/djfksjd/session-alarm/blob/main/SOUND_LICENSE.md).

The duck recording is sourced from the short CC0 audio bundled in
https://github.com/andlo/ovos-skill-sound-like at pinned commit
`77d00889839d6e13af953bf0ee70afb067852aa1`; credits here:
https://github.com/andlo/ovos-skill-sound-like/blob/main/CREDITS.md.

All inputs are decoded with FFmpeg, trimmed and volume-conditioned for
small-hardware playback, then embedded read-only in the firmware. The project
contains the exact upstream commit identities and SHA256 checks where
published, so the build rejects modified/missing sources rather than
silently synthesizing false replacement sounds.

**Audio quality:** these are real recordings, not sounds generated by the
FM-1's oscillators. They are necessarily bandwidth-limited to 16 kHz mono
and may have background noise from the original field recording. For
the bird, we use a **crow**, which is the clearly documented recording;
the on-screen character is a stylized bird.
