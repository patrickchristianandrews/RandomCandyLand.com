❓ HOW TO PLAY · 30-second guideTAP TO OPEN

Keep Trumpagotchi alive. Watch Food, Mood, Energy and Cleanliness. Use the four large buttons to care for your pet:

🍔 FEED — Give him burgers (+25 Food), refill his burger stack and recover some energy. Wait for the feeding cooldown before feeding again.

👏 PRAISE — Improve his mood (+15), but feed, rest and clean him first if those needs are too low.

🧹 CLEAN — Remove wrappers and cartoon messes, boosting Cleanliness (+30).

🛌 REST — Start a nap or wake him. At night, sleeping speeds the clock toward dawn and restores his energy.

Time: A game day lasts roughly five real minutes while awake. Food, Mood and Energy slowly drain. If all three reach zero, the game ends.

Other buttons: Pause stops the clock; Save stores your pet on this device. Sound toggles recorded reactions. Watch News annoys him; Cheer gives a small mood boost when he feels well. Play Him a Melody plays the full original pentatonic tune. You can also tap the five-key piano (C, D, E, G, A) to play individual notes yourself. When he's happy, Trump joins the full tune on bongos. The music can be replayed, but the small mood reward is limited to once a minute. Turn Sound On to hear it. Start Over resets the pet.

Progress: Your pet saves automatically in this browser and does not lose needs while the page is closed.
