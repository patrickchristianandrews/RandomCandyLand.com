❔ HOW TO PLAY

This adaptation follows the Feed · Praise · Clean · Rest care system from the FM-1 release. A day runs in about five minutes. Give him food, tidy cartoon messes, and use Rest when he gets tired. Sleeping at night fast-forwards to morning. Once Food, Mood and Energy all reach zero, the game ends.

Feed restocks burgers. Praise works best after his basic needs are met. Cleaning picks up wrappers and his little accidents. Use Pause or Save when you need to stop. Progress stays in this browser; there is no account or download.

From an actual little pet to a playable web game

This is a browser adaptation of the Trumpagotchi v1.0.0 pet-care game by adamcharleswilsonmusic-code. The original release runs as M-VAVE FM-1 firmware; this independently adapted HTML/JavaScript version reproduces its central pet-care mechanics for phones and browsers. It is not a compiled run of the original firmware, and does not include the FM-1 sequencer. No hardware required.
