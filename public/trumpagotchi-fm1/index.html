❔ HOW TO PLAY Tap to open the game instructions

Keep Trumpagotchi alive. Watch Food, Mood, Energy and Cleanliness. The four large buttons are the FM-1's pet-care controls:

🍔 FEED — Give him burgers (+25 Food), refill his burger stack and recover some energy. Wait for the feeding cooldown before feeding again.

👏 PRAISE — Improve his mood (+15), but feed, rest and clean him first if those needs are too low.

🧹 CLEAN — Remove wrappers and cartoon messes, boosting Cleanliness (+30).

🛌 REST — Start a nap or wake him. At night, sleeping speeds the clock toward dawn and restores his energy.

Time: A game day lasts roughly five real minutes while awake. Food, Mood and Energy slowly drain. If all three reach zero, the game ends.

Other buttons: Pause stops the clock; Save stores your pet on this device. Sound toggles recorded reactions. Watch News annoys him; Cheer gives a small mood boost when he feels well. Start Over resets the pet.

Progress: The game automatically saves in this browser and does not drain your pet's needs while the page is closed. This is a browser adaptation of the FM-1 Trumpagotchi pet game, not the synth firmware.

From an actual little pet to a playable web game

This is a browser adaptation of the Trumpagotchi v1.0.0 pet-care game by adamcharleswilsonmusic-code. The original release runs as M-VAVE FM-1 firmware; this independently adapted HTML/JavaScript version reproduces its central pet-care mechanics for phones and browsers. It is not a compiled run of the original firmware, and does not include the FM-1 sequencer. No hardware required.
