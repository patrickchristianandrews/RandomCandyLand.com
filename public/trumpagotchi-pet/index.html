❓ HOW TO PLAY · 30-second guideTAP TO OPEN ▾
  1. Look after your pet. Press 🍔 FEED, 👏 PRAISE, 🧹 CLEAN or 🛏️ REST to change his mood and watch the animations.
  2. Check his needs. Keep Food, Mood, Energy and Cleanliness from running out. Watch the little status panel beside him.
  3. Play the piano. Tap the five piano keys directly beneath the Feed, Praise, Clean and Rest buttons to play a tune. He dances when he's feeling good.
  4. Generate random cartoon beats. In the Remix Studio, tap Generate Cartoon Beat for a wacky 16-step rhythm. Tap the squares to customize it, press Play, then drop the original voice clips on top—or choose Surprise Trump Drops.
  5. Save it. Your pet progress saves to this browser. The Remix Studio exports your generated cartoon beat and Trump phrases as a WAV file. Adding a song is optional.

🎮 How this version works · game rules & credits

Trumpagotchi 2 is an independent, phone-friendly adaptation of the open-source virtual pet mechanics and artwork from Adam Charles Wilson's Trumpagotchi v1.0.0. Care for him with FEED, PRAISE, CLEAN, and REST. The game includes a fast day/night cycle, snacks, messes, facial expressions, short cooldowns, music, pause, and local saving. The browser version features smoother, higher-resolution character rendering and redesigned touch controls. Voice reactions use complete recordings already available on Random Candy Land, not newly invented quotations.

The original game's code and original artwork are GPL-3.0-only. This HTML/JavaScript port is also offered under GPL-3.0-only, including the full browser port of the original drawing code, with the rest of its complete corresponding source available as the source of this page. Voice-recording rights are separate. The game is unofficial political parody; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Donald Trump, the original creator, or any manufacturer.
