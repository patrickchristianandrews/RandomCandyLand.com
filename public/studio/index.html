01 · SHARED CLOUD (WHEN CONFIGURED)

Community chat

No account, credit card, or API key required from visitors. Share open-model chat backed by the site's free compute allowance. When that allowance runs out, use a local model as a fallback.

Checking free service…

Community messages are processed by the connected provider. Free capacity is limited and shared, with fair-use limits to prevent abuse. This service requires a separate Cloudflare deployment by the site owner.

02 · ON YOUR DEVICE

Local language models

No account and no model charges. Models download to your browser cache, then run on compatible WebGPU hardware. First loads are large and may take a while.

Checking device compatibility…

Preparing…

Local models are smaller and less capable than top cloud models. Performance and availability depend on browser, GPU, memory and downloadable model files.

03 · OPTIONAL CLOUD CONNECTION

Use your own model provider

For more capable chat, connect a service compatible with standard chat completions. Nothing is billed to this website; any charges or limits belong to your own provider account.

Your key is kept in memory in this tab, never saved to local storage. Requests go directly from your browser to the provider, which must allow browser requests (CORS). Only connect providers you trust.

Not connected.

04 · PRIVACY & EXPORT

Keep control of your work

Local chat histories stay on this device. Cloud prompts, if enabled, are sent to the provider you select. Image editing and procedural artwork stay in-browser.

We don't promise unlimited external model usage or pretend browser graphics are photographic model-generated images.
