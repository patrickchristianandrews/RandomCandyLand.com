What is r?

Pearson's correlation coefficient ranges from −1 to +1. A strong relationship in a chart is not evidence that one thing caused the other.

02

Why does this happen?

Unrelated series can move together because of time trends, common outside factors, chance, or because you searched across many pairs.

03

How to challenge it

Inspect first differences, look for confounders, examine sample size, and test a causal explanation. Even a stunning correlation may fall apart.
