🎹 Play the FM-1Choose firmware → Start → Tap keys • Rotate phone for more space

1. Choose a firmware

Select an instrument, sequencer, groovebox, or ambient generator in the full emulator. The embedded view may show only the instrument controls.

2. Tap Start and play

Audio usually starts after a tap. Try the on-screen keys, touch controls or MIDI keyboard. On phones, rotate to landscape for more room.

3. If the embed is blank

Tap “Open full emulator” above. Browser restrictions, audio permissions, and third-party storage can interfere with embedded playback.
