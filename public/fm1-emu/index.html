Authentic third-party emulator

The real FM-1 firmware, in your browser.

Joakim Eriksson's FM-1 emulator simulates the pocket instrument's CPU, LCD, knobs and firmware. For best performance and sound, open it directly rather than inside an iframe competing with another website.

Understanding the MHz warning: MHz is the simulated processor clock, not audio pitch (Hz). If the audio load exceeds 100% or the real-time speed drops below 1×, playback crackles because the emulation can't keep up. Use the emulator's suggested lower clock if available, close developer tools and other heavy apps, or try the developer's standalone desktop version. Some firmware stays too demanding on certain devices, even at a low clock.
