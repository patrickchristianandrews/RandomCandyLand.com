From your answers to your reports

01 · PRIVATE PERSONAL REPORT

Download your results

Take one or both quizzes. Open your results and choose the readable PDF. For a complete cross-quiz interpretation, finish both. Save a separate private JSON backup if you want to restore the answers and regenerate your personal report later.

02 · SELECT FOR SHARING

Choose what your partner can see

Open Reports & Uploads. Select only the topics, psychology themes and answers you want to disclose. Download the selected report file (.json). Your private notes and unselected answers do not go in this file; toy guidance requires extra permission.

03 · PARTNER REPORT

Upload one selected file

Import the selected JSON from Step 2, then create the partner-facing report. Review the contents and save it as a PDF using your browser's Print / Save as PDF option. This is separate from the private report.

04 · COMBINED REPORT

Upload two selected files

Each person prepares their own consent-selected JSON file. Import one as Partner A and the other as Partner B. The combined report presents common themes, differences, relevant question evidence and prompts for talking together.

Which file should I use?

FileUse it forDo not use it for
Personal PDFReading, printing, saving your individual resultsUploading for comparison
Private backup .jsonRestoring your own quiz answers and notes on your deviceSending to a partner or directly comparing two people
Selected report .jsonImporting for a partner-facing or combined reportReplacing your private backup
Partner / combined PDFReviewing and sharing the generated report with permissionRe-importing as structured answers

Why not upload a PDF? A PDF shows the finished text and graphics, but does not reliably preserve the quiz's structured answers, intentional sharing choices or the distinction between fantasy-only and real-life interests. Use the selected JSON export for accurate comparisons.

What the connections mean

Unwrapped maps the questions you actually answered to broad psychology themes and named topics, showing what supports an interpretation. Connections are prompts for reflection rather than proven psychological causes, hidden preferences or clinical findings. A partner comparison uses only sections both people independently chose to disclose. Matching labels do not establish compatibility, agreement or consent.

Privacy and sharing

The quiz and comparison file tools run on your device. A private backup may include sensitive original answers and personal notes, so keep it safe. Exporting a selected report requires a separate choice of shared sections. Everyone involved should agree before sharing files with another person.
