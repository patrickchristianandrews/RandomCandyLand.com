WHAT THE TOY COMPARISONS MEAN

Male, female & shared uses.

The same adult toy is not necessarily suitable for every body area. The report uses the exact compatibility notes and safety instructions shown in the Unwrapped Sex Toy Guide, rather than keeping a separate or outdated list.

Male anatomy

Describes appropriate use for penile or prostate anatomy, and identifies when a product is only suitable for external use or with an expressly anal-safe design.

Female anatomy

Describes vulvar, clitoral and vaginal use, and explains when a product is not designed for that anatomy. A vagina-rated product is not automatically anal-safe.

With a partner

Explains consensual shared or partner-assisted use without assuming that both people must use a particular toy—or that both bodies are compatible with it.

Recommended, or only for reading?

A named interest can support a reading suggestion. Fantasy-only responses remain fantasy-only; an explicit No overrides broad similarities. Everyday psychology can help order already relevant topics but cannot invent a preference.

All examples are educational categories, not sponsored product recommendations or instructions to buy. Product materials, cleaning, lubrication, broad-base protection for anal devices and manufacturer restrictions matter. Personal reports may include this section; partner-facing reports must disclose it separately.

WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR ANSWERS

How the report is built.

The system organizes responses using an internal set of editorial themes. The second quiz adds everyday context; it does not diagnose you or independently prove specific sexual interests. Both quizzes contribute to a single consolidated interpretation rather than conflicting reports.

STEP 01

Your direct choices

You identify particular interests, selected activities, boundaries and whether a choice is fantasy-only, curious or something you might explore.

STEP 02

Everyday context

Nonsexual responses address trust, communication, sensory preferences, emotional needs, rituals, anticipation and roles.

STEP 03

Shared theme map

Answers are grouped into the same broad themes. Report sections show the actual selected answers that support each described connection.

STEP 04

Interpret, then choose

You receive the detailed private report, and may separately make an edited partner copy or write your own new-partner introduction.

Why an interest is included

Direct selections are distinguished from broad matches. When you specifically choose an interest, the report explains its description, associated themes, supporting answers and optional questions. An inferred topic is visibly labeled as an unconfirmed suggestion.

How percentages are calculated

Relative theme-fit numbers summarize the site's editorial mapping of the answers you selected. They are not statistical probabilities of having an interest, clinical assessment scores, measures of compatibility or a guarantee you will enjoy an activity.

What happens when quizzes disagree

Different emphases are recorded instead of being erased. A difference may mean different contexts, unclear questions or simply separate preferences. It does not require a psychological explanation and must not override a stated boundary.

Where suggestions come from

General interest suggestions may use shared themes for optional reading. Toy suggestions are more conservative: they require an actual named sexual interest or preference, preserve fantasy-only choices and explicitly respect Not for me answers. They never assume a person wants a product based on gender or psychology.

WHAT RESULTS ACTUALLY MEAN

An example of responsible interpretation.

Direct answer: Someone reports liking specific, sincere praise.

Nonsexual answer: The same person values recognition that feels genuine in everyday relationships.

Report interpretation: Both answers share an appreciation or affirmation theme. The personal report can explain how feeling seen may matter and pose questions for reflection. It cannot conclude that one response caused the other, identify a childhood origin or predict a particular intimate preference.

Possible reading suggestion: An affectionate ritual involving appreciation may be worth learning about, clearly labeled unconfirmed until the person chooses it for themselves.

Other interpretive themes may involve sensation, aesthetics, trust, personal space, novelty, imagination or anticipation. Each topic should provide concrete definitions, answer-level evidence, the limits of the interpretation, and specific conversation ideas. It should never conflate fantasy with consent or curiosity with intent.

YOUR WORDS · YOUR BOUNDARIES

What stays private, and what you control.

Private personal report

The complete personal export can include original answers and notes. It is generated locally in your browser, and the downloadable PDF stays wherever you save it. Treat it as a sensitive personal document and choose carefully if you later share it.

Optional partner reports

Sex toy comparisons and recommendations remain out unless you enable their separate disclosure. The new-partner introduction is nonsexual by default. Quiz answers, intimate topics, and toy material are different opt-in choices. Nothing is automatically shared.

These tools are for self-reflection and discussion, not diagnosis, treatment, screening for trauma, determination of sexual orientation or proof of a preference's origins. Each person's own account, comfort and freely given consent take priority.
