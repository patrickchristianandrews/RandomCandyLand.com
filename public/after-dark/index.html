A PRIVATE, TOPIC-AWARE REFERENCE GUIDE

Ask the Atlas.

Ask directly about an adult interest, its possible appeal, your relationship, consent or communicating with a partner. Atlas now prioritizes the subject of your question instead of recommending an unrelated topic.

You can ask specific questions. For example: “Why might I enjoy being the receiving partner with a strap-on?”, “What is switching?”, or “How do I tell my partner what I want?” I can discuss possibilities without guessing your identity or diagnosing you.

Atlas is a locally programmed educational guide, not a generative AI chatbot or therapist. Your questions are processed on your device, not sent to a server. It cannot know the cause of any individual's preferences. See Resources for further reading.

Practical grounding

Start with care.

Define your limits

Use yes / maybe / no lists. A “maybe” means permission to discuss—not permission to act.

Consent stays active

Anyone can decline or stop at any time. Relationship status, costumes or roleplay do not change that.

Keep fantasy separate

Some themes are enjoyable only as fiction. That is valid. Never involve unknowing bystanders, minors or people unable to consent.

Acknowledge risk

Choking, breath restriction, unsafe restraints, sharps and impaired consent pose serious dangers. This guide does not teach these activities.
