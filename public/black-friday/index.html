Make sure your savings really stack
  1. Find the exact item and an eligible coupon first.
  2. Choose one shopping portal; do not add competing portal rates together.
  3. Check whether the coupon, gift-card payment, membership or product category changes eligibility.
  4. Activate the chosen portal and follow its checkout instructions. Another rewards extension may interfere with tracking.
  5. Keep the receipt. Cashback is a later reward, subject to tracking, approval, returns and exclusions.
