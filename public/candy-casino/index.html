Choose a game: Poker, High Card or Dice. Play five rounds against Candybot, or connect to one friend. All chips are virtual; no money or prizes.

Poker: get five cards, select up to three to exchange, then compare hands. The strongest standard poker hand wins. Your friend's cards remain hidden until the showdown.

Playing online: select Play a Friend, create a room, and share the link. If you receive a link, open it to join. Keep both browsers open. Cloudflare connects the two browsers through a dedicated game relay, so no direct phone-to-phone connection is required.
