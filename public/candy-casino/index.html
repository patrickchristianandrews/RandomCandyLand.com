Every game has two competitors. Play against the computer instantly or invite a real person from anywhere. Choose five-card draw poker, High Card Clash or Gummy Dice Duel; win candy crowns and free, fictional chips. No purchases, deposits, real money, or prizes.

Poker: both players receive five private cards, then independently choose up to three to replace. After both draw, the best standard five-card poker hand wins. Ties are possible. Face-down opponent cards remain hidden until the showdown. Each match is five rounds.

Online: select Play a Friend, create a room and send the link, or open a friend's link and join. Both devices must stay online. Connections use the PeerJS public signaling service and WebRTC; network restrictions may interfere. You can switch back to Computer Opponent anytime.
