🍭 How to play

  • Watch the candy buttons flash and play their little tunes.
  • Tap the candies in the same order. Each level adds a new candy!
  • Win stars every round and see how high you can go.
  • One wrong candy ends the round. Start a new game to beat your best!

Use keyboard 1, 2, 3, 4 too. Replay the pattern once per level when you need a little help.
