🍭 HOW TO PLAY

1. Look at the giant candy at the top of your board. 2. Tap that exact candy in the grid. 3. Repeat as fast as you can to build combo multipliers. Wrong taps cost points and break your combo!

2-Player Duel: Compete simultaneously on the same screen. Each player gets an equally generated set of puzzles, and the highest score when time runs out wins.

Friend Challenge: Send your link to a friend. Both of you play the exact same seeded set of puzzles on your own devices, then compare scores. This is an asynchronous challenge, not a live internet match.

🎯 Scoring tricks

Every correct pick increases your streak; every three hits increases the multiplier up to x6. At seven consecutive hits, you enter SUGAR FEVER for double points! Every fifth consecutive hit adds a second to the clock (up to 50 seconds). Misses cost 25 points.
