✺ Candy Pulse · TV

Open RandomCandyLand.com/music-visualizer/ on your phone, start your music, tap Cast / TV → Create TV room, then enter the code below. The TV renders the moving visualizer locally for a smoother picture.

Use the same Wi-Fi for the most reliable results. A TV browser with Canvas and WebSockets is required.

No video decoding is needed. If your TV browser supports WebRTC, you may also enable the streamed audio. Standard Chromecast devices should use phone screen mirroring instead.
