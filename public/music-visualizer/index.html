📺 Put Candy Pulse on your TV

New low-latency TV Sync: Your phone sends only a lightweight live music spectrum while the TV draws the visuals itself, avoiding video-stream lag. Compatible smart-TV browsers can display visuals even without WebRTC; WebRTC adds optional TV audio. Standard Chromecast devices still use Android screen mirroring.

Google TV / Chromecast · Android

  1. Connect your phone and TV to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the Google Home app and select the television or Chromecast device.
  3. Tap Cast my screen → Cast screen (labels may vary).
  4. Return to Pulse, tap Full screen, and play music or the demo.

The regular Google Cast media button cannot directly send an animated web canvas; Android screen mirroring is the supported solution for Chromecast without a custom registered receiver.

✨ Recommended · Fast TV Sync (smart-TV browser)

Play a track or start the microphone, then create a room. On the TV open RandomCandyLand.com/music-visualizer/tv.html, type the 20-character code and connect. The TV generates full-screen visuals locally instead of decoding video. A TV browser with WebRTC can also receive your music when supported.

Start a TV room to pair your television.

Visuals need a TV browser with Canvas and WebSockets. TV audio additionally needs WebRTC support and may require a tap on the television. Spectrum data is sent through the Cloudflare signaling relay; uploaded music files stay on your phone. For Chromecast without a TV browser, use Android screen mirroring.

For standard Chromecast: On Android open Google Home → your Cast device → Cast screen, then return to Pulse and enter Full screen. On desktop Chrome use ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Cast tab. Native one-tap Cast of custom visuals requires a registered Google Cast receiver app.

🌈 Customize the visuals

Classic visualizer effects now include 3D star tunnels, psychedelic plasma, circular spectrum bars and real audio waveforms. Bass drives depth and beat flashes; mids morph geometry and treble illuminates fine detail. Auto Journey switches presets approximately every 12 seconds.
