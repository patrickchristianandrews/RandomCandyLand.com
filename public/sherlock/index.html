HOW IT WORKS

Sherlock, in your browser.

This app runs the authentic open-source Sherlock command-line program on your server, then displays its results here.

01

Search names

Enter one or more usernames. Sherlock checks supported networks for public account matches. {?} expands username variations.

02

Adjust scope

Choose exact site names, search all sites, change the timeout, or enable the same advanced site options exposed by Sherlock.

03

Review evidence

Found usernames are not verified identities. Open the linked profiles and examine them before drawing conclusions.

04

Export findings

Download raw-style Sherlock output as CSV, XLSX, TXT, or JSON. Scans run on the server, not in your browser.
